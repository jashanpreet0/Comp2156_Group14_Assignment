George Brown College Provides various kind of courses for international as well as domestic students. It has three different main campuses.
They offer business, technology, health science, hospitality and community service programs. The classrooms are very contemporary and complete with labs and the newest equipment. Another huge thing about GBC is its relation to real companies. There are lots of programs which have internships, co-ops and projects where you can experience a real job. GBC is preparing graduates to smash it in the real world confident and professional, with the hands-on vibe and a supportive-vibe.
