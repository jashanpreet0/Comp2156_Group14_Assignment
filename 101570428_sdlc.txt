The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is nothing but the process map that we operate by to develop software. It begins by planning and then maintains it once the product is out there. Previously, the development and operations were absolutely distinct, so the development had much procrastination. DevOps repairs that by stimulating perpetual communicating and automation in the entire cycle. Through DevOps, the teams will be able to roll out updates more frequently and squash bugs quicker. The result? Improved software and more pleased users. The proper SDLC with DevOps can truly streamline the entire dev process and make it more efficient. Clear communication during each phase of SDLC helps avoid confusion and reduces development risks.
